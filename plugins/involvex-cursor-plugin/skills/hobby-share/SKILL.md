# Hobby share

Hobby constraint: no ads, no paid boosts, no new paid services. Draft only; the human posts.

## When to run

After shipping a `ROADMAP.md` item that a person can install, clone, try, or demo.

## Steps

1. If `ROADMAP.md` has no open share-related item under `## Later` for this work, append one, for example:
   `- [ ] Share <thing>: free announcement draft in SHARE.md`
2. Write or update `SHARE.md` in the repo (include it in the PR):

```markdown
# Share draft

## Audience
Who might care (one short line).

## Announcement
2–4 sentences. Link to the repo or demo. No hype claims you cannot back.

## Free channels (pick one)
- GitHub README / Discussions / release notes
- Relevant Discord or forum (no spam)
- Personal social post (human posts this)

## Do not
- Paid ads, boosts, or new SaaS accounts
- Cold-message strangers at scale
```

3. Do not post, email, or publish outside the repository. Leave the draft for the human.

## Output

Confirm the Later item and the `SHARE.md` path. Remind the user they post it themselves.
